# Argus phases and milestone approvals

Core promise: Argus tells you when your research needs to change, and shows you
exactly why. Governing direction: user-supplied phase plan, accepted 2026-09-15.

## Operating agreement

- Work one causal problem per implementation slice, with separate commits and
  rollback boundaries. Observe, trace, prove, measure, implement, test, deploy,
  validate, then close.
- Stop and report at each milestone. Do not interpret a status report, elapsed
  time, a passing deployment, or silence as approval to advance a major phase.
- Phase 1 is authorized to begin. Phases 2-4 require explicit user approval.
- Preserve existing EA1, EA2a, ET1a, and RC2 closures unless new regression evidence
  warrants a separately documented reopening. OP1.2 and historical remediation
  are outside these slices.
- Human approval establishes benchmark truth. AI assistance, proposed labels,
  regression fixtures, and current engine output never count as approved truth.
- No new major product surfaces during Phase 1. Do not add agreement machinery,
  another memory engine, portfolio risk, alerts, or additional providers here.

## Phase 2: Essential research workflow, not yet authorized

Use Saved plus the existing archive/change ledger for followed subjects,
since-last-review changes, durable notes, thesis, evidence changes, watch
conditions, review history, and cross-device persistence. Define what the system
actually computes before displaying support/contradiction or triggered conditions.

Exit: a user completes the research loop across devices without losing their view
or confusing observed facts with inference. Validate ownership/isolation and
graceful failure. Stop for approval before Phase 3.

## Phase 3: Ten-user pilot, not yet authorized

Target four active investors, three analysts, and three finance/PM-style users.
Observe task replacement, discoveries, trust, unprompted return, time saved, and
remaining research gaps. Obtain authorization before contacting participants.
Do not substitute aggregate engagement for observed task value. Stop and review
results before approving expansion.

## Phase 4: Evidence-led expansion, not yet authorized

Select holdings, portfolio exposure, notifications, documents, earnings workflows,
collaboration, exports, or institutional controls only from validated needs.
No blanket authorization to build this list is implied.

## Milestone report format

State status, causal scope, revision, before/after evidence, tests, production
controls, known limits, rollback, and remaining conditions. Identify the next
milestone/phase and its approval state. A blocked milestone stays open.
